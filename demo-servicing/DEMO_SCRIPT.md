# RolledCo Demo Script

Seven beats, about two and a half minutes. Record at 1440 by 900. Click
Reset Demo in the bottom left before each take so the Review rows and the
Outbox start clean. Start on the Closing Binder screen.

Spoken lines are short on purpose. Pause on each screen long enough for the
viewer to read the numbers.

---

## Beat 1. The Deal (0:00 to 0:20)

**On screen:** Closing Binder. Hover over the document list. Do not click yet.

**Say:**
"This is a small business acquisition. Northgate bought Brightline Heating and Air for 2.4 million, closed July 15th. Most of the money moved at closing. What's left is the part nobody services: two seller notes, a consulting fee, three retention bonuses, a holdback, and the bank's reporting covenants."

"RolledCo is the servicing layer for that money. It reads the closing documents and builds one ledger. It never holds or moves funds."

## Beat 2. Upload and Extract (0:20 to 0:40)

**On screen:** Click **Extract Obligations**. Let the four agents run. When the summary appears, pause on it.

**Say:**
"Eleven documents go in. Four agents run in sequence. The Reader pulls every obligation out of each document. Compliance cross-checks them against each other. Calculation works out the dates and accruals. Servicing builds the ledger."

"Twelve obligations, two flags. Let's look at the flags."

## Beat 3. Review and the Two Caught Conflicts (0:40 to 1:05)

**On screen:** Click **Open Review**. Point to the Seller Note B row, then click **View Source** on it. Show the quoted clause and the conflicting clause from the purchase agreement. Close the drawer. Scroll to the Carol Dempsey row.

**Say:**
"Every row has a confidence score and a link to the exact clause it came from."

"Here's the first catch. The seller note says the first payment is August 15th, 2028. The purchase agreement says September 15th. One month apart, and nobody noticed at closing."

"Second catch. Three retention bonus letters. Two have a forfeiture clause if the employee leaves. The office manager's letter doesn't. As written, that bonus is owed whether she stays or not."

"A person decides what to do. Approve, edit, or reject."

## Beat 4. The Ledger (1:05 to 1:30)

**On screen:** Click **Ledger**. Pause on the three totals. Then sweep across the timeline from left to right. Hover a Seller Note B marker so the tooltip shows.

**Say:**
"This is the ledger. Ten years, one row per instrument, every payment on the date it's due. The shaded bands are standby periods where the bank says no payment can be made."

"Due this month, due in the next twelve months, and what's outstanding to each party. The seller is owed 339 thousand across four instruments, and most of it can't be paid for years."

## Beat 5. The Blocked Standby Payment (1:30 to 1:50)

**On screen:** Click **Monitor**. Click **Schedule Payment** on Seller Note A. Pause on the crimson verdict and the quoted clause.

**Say:**
"Say the buyer wants to pay down Seller Note A early. RolledCo blocks it and tells you why: the note is on full standby for the life of the SBA loan. It quotes the clause from the note and from the standby agreement."

"The consulting fee isn't on standby, so that one goes through."

## Beat 6. Month-End Performance Note Math (1:50 to 2:15)

**On screen:** Click **Run Month End**. Pause on the bars, then read down the math table. Point to the two crimson rows.

**Say:**
"Month end reads the P&L. Seller Note B is a performance note: if trailing revenue at month 24 is under 3.1 million, the principal drops dollar for dollar, with a 60 thousand floor."

"Six months in, revenue annualizes to 3.055 million. That's a 45 thousand shortfall, so the projected principal is 75 thousand instead of 120. The math is laid out line by line, so the buyer and the seller are looking at the same numbers before anyone argues about them."

## Beat 7. Approve in the Outbox (2:15 to 2:35)

**On screen:** Click **Outbox**. Scroll the consulting payment instruction. Click **Approve**. Show the status change. Then flip the **Seller View** toggle for two seconds.

**Say:**
"The servicing agent drafts the paperwork: this month's payment instruction, a statement for the seller, and the bank's quarterly cover note. A person approves. That marks it ready for the buyer's bank. RolledCo doesn't send it and doesn't move the money."

"And the seller gets a read-only view of exactly what he's owed and when."

"That's RolledCo. One ledger for everything owed after close, with a person approving every payment."

---

## Timing Notes

- Beats 1 through 3 carry the setup; keep them moving.
- Let the extraction animation run without talking over the last second.
- The Monitor screen has the two strongest moments. Give each verdict a full breath.
- If a take runs long, cut the Seller View flip from Beat 7.
