Re: Bookkeeping and IOLTA Trust Account Services - Calendar Years 2024 and 2025

Dear Ms. Desrosiers:

This letter sets forth the terms under which Harbor Bookkeeping LLC will
provide services to Beacon Hill Law Group, P.C. for the period January 1,
2024 through December 31, 2025 (the "Term").

SERVICES

1. Operating Account. Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliation of the firm's
   operating account at Bank of New Hampshire, with financial statements
   delivered by the 15th of the following month.

2. IOLTA Trust Account. Monthly three-way reconciliation of the firm's IOLTA
   account (bank statement, trust ledger, individual client ledgers) in
   accordance with NH Supreme Court Rule 50. The reconciliation report will be
   delivered to the Managing Partner for signature by the 10th of the
   following month. Any discrepancy will be reported the same business day it
   is identified.

3. Billing Support. We will export approved time from Clio on the 1st of each
   month and generate client invoices for your review by the 3rd.

4. Annual. Preparation of the firm's 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC forms by January
   31, and a year-end package to your CPA by March 1. We will also prepare the
   annual NH Supreme Court Rule 50 trust account certification data by the
   August 1 deadline.

FEES

$1,350 per month for items 1-3, invoiced on the 1st of each month, due on
receipt. Annual items at $125 per hour.

TERM

This engagement expires on December 31, 2025. Continuation of services beyond
that date requires a new engagement letter. We will contact you in November
2025 regarding renewal.

Very truly yours,

Ruth Callahan
Owner, Harbor Bookkeeping LLC
