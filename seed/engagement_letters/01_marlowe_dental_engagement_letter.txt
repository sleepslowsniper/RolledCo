Thank you for choosing Harbor Bookkeeping LLC ("we", "us") to provide bookkeeping
services to Marlowe Family Dental, PLLC ("the Client"). This letter confirms the
terms of our engagement.

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

   a) Monthly bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online, including categorization of all
      bank and credit card transactions and reconciliation of the following
      accounts: Citizens Bank operating (x4471), Citizens Bank payroll (x4489),
      Chase Ink Business card (x9920).
   b) Monthly financial package (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, A/R aging)
      delivered by the 15th of the following month.
   c) Quarterly New Hampshire Business Profits Tax / Business Enterprise Tax
      estimate reminders (we do not prepare the return; your CPA, Fitzgerald &
      Lowe, does).
   d) Annual preparation and filing of Forms 1099-NEC for contractors, due
      January 31 each year. Client to provide W-9s by January 10.

2. FEES

   Fixed monthly fee of $1,200.00, invoiced on the 1st of each month, due net 15.
   Fee review annually each January.

3. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

   Client will provide read-only bank access via QuickBooks bank feeds and will
   send receipts for any transaction over $250 through the Dext app within 30
   days. Merchant statements (Dentrix Pay) to be uploaded to the shared Dropbox
   folder by the 5th of each month.

4. TERM

   This engagement begins January 1, 2025 and continues until terminated by
   either party with 30 days' written notice.
