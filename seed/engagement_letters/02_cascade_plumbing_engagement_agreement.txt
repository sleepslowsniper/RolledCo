BOOKKEEPING AND PAYROLL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of March 1, 2024 between HARBOR BOOKKEEPING LLC,
a New Hampshire limited liability company ("Provider"), and CASCADE PLUMBING &
HEATING, INC., a New Hampshire corporation ("Client").

RECITALS
Client operates a residential and commercial plumbing business with approximately
11 employees. Client wishes to engage Provider for bookkeeping and payroll
administration.

ARTICLE I - SERVICES
1.1 Bookkeeping. Provider shall maintain Client's general ledger in QuickBooks
    Online, reconcile all bank and credit accounts monthly, and deliver monthly
    financial statements no later than the 20th day of the following month.
1.2 Payroll. Provider shall process Client's payroll on a BI-WEEKLY basis
    (every other Friday) through Gusto. Client shall submit approved timesheets
    no later than 12:00 noon on the Wednesday preceding each pay date. Provider
    shall remit federal and state payroll tax deposits and file Forms 941, NH
    unemployment (NHES) quarterly reports, and annual Forms W-2/W-3.
1.3 Job Costing. Provider shall maintain class tracking by job for jobs
    exceeding $5,000 in contract value.
1.4 Year-End. Provider shall prepare Forms 1099-NEC for subcontractors and
    deliver a year-end package to Client's tax preparer by February 15.
1.5 Workers' Compensation Audit. Provider shall prepare payroll summaries for
    Client's annual workers' compensation audit (policy year ends June 30).

ARTICLE II - COMPENSATION
2.1 Bookkeeping fee: $850.00 per month.
2.2 Payroll fee: $95.00 per payroll run plus $6.00 per employee per run.
2.3 Year-end 1099 preparation: $15.00 per form, minimum $75.00.
2.4 Invoices are issued on the last business day of each month and are due
    within 30 days.

ARTICLE III - TERM AND TERMINATION
3.1 Initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, automatically
    renewing for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives sixty
    (60) days' written notice prior to the end of the then-current term.

ARTICLE IV - MISCELLANEOUS
4.1 Provider is not responsible for penalties arising from late submission of
    timesheets or client-caused delays.
4.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Hampshire.
